About this tour

Experience the magic of Bali's lush jungles and spiritual heritage on this curated 5-day journey. From the emerald-green rice terraces of Tegallalang to the serene water temples of Tirta Empul, you'll discover why Ubud is known as the island's cultural heart. Includes private transportation, boutique villa stays, and authentic culinary experiences.

Tour Itinerary

Step 1: Welcome to Ubud

Arrival at Ngurah Rai Airport, private transfer to your villa, and traditional welcome dinner with Balinese dance.

Step 2: Sacred Water & Rice Fields

Spiritual cleansing at Tirta Empul temple followed by a guided trek through the iconic Tegallalang Rice Terrace.

Step 3: Jungle Swing & Hidden Waterfall

Morning adventure at Bali Swing for panoramic views and afternoon cooling down at Tegenungan Waterfall.
